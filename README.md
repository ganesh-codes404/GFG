# Games for Groups

> **Instant multiplayer games for friends. No downloads. No accounts. Just join and play.**

## Overview

Games for Groups is a browser-based platform designed for friend groups to quickly create a room and play multiplayer games together.

Instead of downloading multiple apps or creating accounts, players simply enter a nickname, join a room with a code, and start playing.

Whether you're on a Discord call, hanging out in person, or killing time during a break, Games for Groups makes group gaming effortless.

---

## Vision

Playing games with friends should take seconds—not minutes.

Games for Groups removes unnecessary friction by eliminating installations, sign-ups, and complicated setup. Our goal is to become the go-to platform whenever a group of friends asks:

> **"What should we play?"**
